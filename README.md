# Healthcare Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes healthcare data to identify patterns in patient demographics, age groups, medical conditions, diagnostic test results, medication distribution, and insurance providers.

The data was analyzed and visualized using Microsoft Excel, with the findings presented through an interactive dashboard and PowerPoint presentation.

## Key Metrics

* **Total Records Analyzed:** 849
* **Adults:** 613 (72.2%)
* **Youth:** 208 (24.5%)
* **Teenagers:** 28 (3.3%)

## Patient Demographics & Age Groups

The analysis examined the distribution of healthcare records across different age groups.

Adults accounted for the largest proportion of the records, representing **72.2%** of the total sample, followed by Youth at **24.5%** and Teenagers at **3.3%**.

The medication analysis also showed that Adults accounted for the majority of medication prescriptions, followed by Youth and Teenagers.

## Medical Condition Insights

The analysis identified the following distribution across major medical conditions:

| Medical Condition | Total Cases |
| ----------------- | ----------: |
| Diabetes          |         151 |
| Arthritis         |         146 |
| Asthma            |         142 |
| Cancer            |         142 |
| Hypertension      |         135 |
| Obesity           |         133 |

Diabetes was the most frequently recorded medical condition, with **151 cases**, followed by Arthritis with **146 cases**.

### Age Distribution by Condition

* **Diabetes:** 151 cases — 104 Adults, 42 Youth, 4 Teenagers
* **Arthritis:** 146 cases — 110 Adults, 31 Youth, 5 Teenagers
* **Asthma:** 142 cases — 99 Adults, 37 Youth, 6 Teenagers
* **Cancer:** 142 cases — 101 Adults, 37 Youth, 4 Teenagers
* **Hypertension:** 135 cases — 96 Adults, 36 Youth, 3 Teenagers
* **Obesity:** 133 cases — 103 Adults, 25 Youth, 5 Teenagers

## Diagnostic Results

The analysis also examined diagnostic test results by gender.

Among female records, **Abnormal** results represented the highest category, followed by **Inconclusive** and **Normal** results.

Among male records, the distribution was more balanced, with **Normal** results representing the highest category.

## Insurance Provider Distribution

The records were distributed across major insurance providers, including:

* Aetna
* Blue Cross
* Cigna
* Medicare
* UnitedHealthcare

The analysis showed a relatively balanced distribution across the insurance providers, with each provider covering records across multiple medical conditions.

## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard brings together the major findings from the analysis, including:

* Patient age-group distribution
* Medication distribution by age group
* Medical condition prevalence
* Medical conditions by age group
* Gender and diagnostic test results
* Insurance provider distribution
